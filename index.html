02 — ENGINEERING

University Projects:

01

Alcohol Removal Process

PROCESS DESIGN

Completed a group project where we designed a process to reduce the alcohol content of beer to below 0.5% while also recovering and purifying the extracted ethanol. This project consisted of writing a report including a PFD, P&ID, HAZOP, Economic & Environmental Analysis and data sheets, as well as detailed descriptions of the process throughout.

For this project we used:

  • Aspen Plus: to model the process and determine the optimal operating conditions for each unit operation.
  • Siemens NX: to design the PFDs and P&IDs for the process.
  • Microsoft Excel: to perform calculations and analyse the data.
02

Propranolol Reactor Optimisation

REACTION ENGINEERING

Modelled a 10 m³ batch reactor to investigate how operating strategy, temperature and reactant ratios affect product conversion and selectivity in propranolol production. The project identified optimal operating conditions to maximise desired products, minimise by-products and meet the annual production target.

For this project we used:

  • Matlab: to generate the graphs and analyse the data.
  • Microsoft Excel: to input data.
